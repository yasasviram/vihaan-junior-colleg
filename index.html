Programs

Academic Streams

M.P.C. (Engineering)

Target: IIT-JEE (Mains & Adv), BITSAT, NITs

Our Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry divisions utilize strictly "Concept-Based Teaching". We systematically dismantle rote memorization, replacing it with advanced analytical problem-solving skills tested via weekly CBTs.

Bi.P.C. (Medical)

Target: NEET, AIIMS, JIPMER

Comprehensive, high-yield Biology lectures integrated with rigorous Physics and Chemistry mechanics. Our faculty ensures an exceptional student-to-teacher ratio for highly personalized conceptual clarity.
